# table-tennis-ranking-analysis

Overview

Analysis of table tennis rankings from Jan 2010 to Oct 2026. Using this data to analyse for trends connected to the specifications of the players (age, handedness, country, and so on.)
This project explores this topic using Python and PowerBI.

Dataset

Rankings were derived from this site: https://clazychen.github.io/Table-Tennis-Rankings/
From Jan 2010 to Oct 2026, this totaled over 40,000 player entries over the course of 202 months.

Findings

Chinese dominance is no joke, with the top 8 peaks all belonging to chinese players. We however can observe over the last three years, the chinese share of the top 20 has been declining.
Left-handed players are vastly over-represented at the top compared to the population average. This suggests there is advantage in being left-handed.
Average ranking trends down until the age of 29. While the average age of the top 20 is roughly 26, it suggests that the peak of a table tennis player is in the late 20s, which is later than most sports. 
The average age of joining the top20 is lower than the average age of exiting the top20, but it is not by as much as one might imagine. This may be attributed due to a lot of the players who join the top20 are ones who leave it shortly thereafter, as they are on the fringe.
